# Karan Number Finder Pro AI (v1.0)

**Developer / Copyright:** © 2026 Hardik Kumar. All Rights Reserved.

A privacy-first, AI-powered phone verification, OCR photo analysis, spam/scam risk detection, and bilingual (Hindi + English) AI security assistant built with **Dark 3D Glass** and **White Glass** interfaces.

## Key Modules
1. **Number Lookup:** International E.164 validation (`libphonenumber`), country/calling code & type detection, honest public caller/business verification, and AI fraud risk scoring.
2. **AI Photo Scanner:** Zero-permission Photo Picker & permission-gated Camera capture with on-device ML Kit OCR + Gemini Multimodal Vision (zero facial identification).
3. **AI Spam Detector:** Phishing, KYC scam, UPI fraud, and Digital Arrest detection with bilingual English & Hindi explanations.
4. **AI Assistant:** Streaming Hindi & English AI security copilot powered by Firebase AI Logic (`gemini-3.8-flash`) + App Check.
5. **Business Lookup:** Public business & official Indian/global helpline verification (`1930`, `112`, etc.).
6. **Search History:** Encrypted local Room database (`AES-256-GCM`), individual deletion, and native PDF export & sharing.
7. **Security Center:** HTTPS + App Check status, Privacy Dashboard, sliding-window Rate Limiter, Permission Manager, and optional 4-digit PIN + Biometric lock.
8. **Settings:** Dark 3D Glass, White Glass, and Cyber Aurora themes, glass intensity slider, language selector, and built-in legal/API documentation.
